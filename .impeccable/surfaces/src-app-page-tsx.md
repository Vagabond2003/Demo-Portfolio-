## Direction contract

THESIS: The portfolio is a garment tech pack for software: each project is specified, approved and shipped like a production order from a Bangladeshi factory floor; it refuses the dark dev-portfolio hero, skill bars and card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Marker-paper white with a printed teal coordinate grid; a woven-label navy field with ivory thread lettering and folded, stitched ends; kraft hang tags and corrugated carton board; technical flats in navy line ink with dashed topstitch seams and numbered callouts; stamp red reserved only for LIVE approval and the order action. Archivo across its width axis (condensed spec labels, expanded woven name), Big Shoulders Stencil for carton marks, Anek Bangla for বাংলা.

STORY: A client meets a precise maker, reads what each product does in one sentence, sees it stamped approved and live, believes he builds to spec, and emails an order.

FIRST VIEWPORT: One tech-pack cover sheet on marker paper: thin running header strip; the woven label carrying his name spans ~60% of the width at top-left; the offer line "Built to spec. Shipped to production." sits under it with a one-paragraph pitch; a ruled spec table (lines, samples, origin, status) on the right; a red APPROVED stamp overlaps the label's edge; a kraft hang tag on a string swings from the label's right end and is the email action.

FORM: Tech Pack — Made in Bangladesh; my #1 ranked grounded candidate (taken as IMPECCABLE’S PICK on re-roll 1); seed key 1378381e. Signature interaction: the draggable, scroll-swayed hang tag; motion grammar: woven row-by-row label reveal, flats drawing on with seam lines, stamp slam on approval, colorway swatches swapping real screenshots (EN/বাংলা, light/dark).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
